# Overview

Triton X-100 is a non-ionic [Detergent](../detergent/spec.md). Here it is used for the effect that makes a detergent a hazard elsewhere: added deliberately, at a dose that dissolves a bilayer, it releases everything a liposome holds.

**It is the positive lysis control.** [Colorimetric Readout](../../processes/colorimetric-readout/main.md) calls for it in the same plate read as the sample, so the signal from a fully released population sits beside the signal being measured. A readout that cannot reach that level has not failed to trigger; it has failed to report.

**The same molecule is a contaminant at a lower dose.** This Module and [Tween 80](../detergent-tween-80/spec.md) differ in how they arrive rather than in what they do: one is added on purpose and one comes in with a protein stock.

# Expected Behavior

At the lysis dose, a liposome population releases its contents and the readout reaches the level a fully released sample gives. No page here records the concentration used, and no run here measures the dose at which release begins.
