# Overview

Preparing lipids combines the molecules a bilayer is made from into one mixture, in the proportions that mixture is specified at. It is what produces the thing every encapsulation route takes as its first argument.

It is the step before a boundary exists. Nothing here is closed around anything: the product is a mixture of lipids, and a route under [Encapsulation](../encapsulate/main.md) is what turns that mixture into a compartment.

:::{attention} 🚧 Draft
This page is written to close a gap rather than to document a bench procedure. See the Protocol section.
:::

# Protocol

No protocol is recorded here yet. The attested preparations are on the Module pages, because each states its own mixture:

- [Membrane: POPC](../../modules/membrane-popc/spec.md) gives 99.15 mol% POPC and 0.85 mol% DSPE-PEG2000, from stocks in chloroform, as a 3.5x batch.
- [Membrane: POPC/Chol](../../modules/membrane-popc-chol/spec.md) and [Membrane: POPC/Chol (9:1)](../../modules/membrane-popc-chol-9-1/spec.md) give three-lipid mixtures by mol%.

Each is a composition rather than a procedure. What a bench user does with the mixture differs by route, and that belongs to the route.

# Requirements

The proportions must be stated as a molar ratio rather than as volumes. A volume only means something next to a total, and the total belongs to the batch rather than to the mixture.
