# Overview

<!-- gen:position -->
**Position.** Refines [Nucleic Acid](../nucleic-acid/spec.md). Refined by nothing on this branch.
<!-- /gen:position -->

A class: a Module whose subject is an RNA molecule.

Every member is a nucleic acid polymer with a ribose backbone, and is identified by its sequence rather than by what the sequence does. The class fixes what they are made of, which is what decides how a nuclease treats them.

A Module that produces RNA is not a member. A transcript is a product of the step that transcribes it.

# Expected Behavior

A member is read or translated and is degraded by any RNase that reaches it. Protecting it is what an RNase inhibitor is for.
