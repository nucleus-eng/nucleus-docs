# Overview

Making produces a component that was not among its operands. That is what separates it from assembling: an assembly puts things in one compartment and every one of them survives as a factor of the result, while a make consumes what it is given and yields something new.

Its instances are the reagent preparations, protein purification, and expression from a template.
