## Contributors Graph

An interactive map from contributors to the DevNotes they authored to the Module, Process, and Implementation pages those DevNotes support. Nodes are filled circles, colored by type: **contributors** (green), **DevNotes** (purple), **modules** (blue), **processes** (brown), **implementations** (orange). A contributor appears here only if their name above matches a published DevNote's author name exactly — many contributors listed above have supported this work in other ways (review, lab work not yet written up, tooling) and will not appear as a node.

**Click a node** to open its detail panel. **Click the background** to close it. **Click a node type in the legend** to show or hide it. To regenerate after new DevNotes are published or `scripts/devnote-associations.yml` changes: `python3 scripts/build-contributors-graph.py`.
